Architecture

Corrective RAG pipeline

Research and document-backed Revise and Review workflows anonymize input before retrieval. Generate without a document sends the drafting prompt directly to Gemini and skips retrieval, so do not include confidential or identifying information in that prompt. A second regex PII firewall gates every Supermemory write.
