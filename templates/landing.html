How it works

A corrective RAG pipeline

Argus embeds queries with Cohere Embed v4, searches an 11,266-passage Neon pgvector index, reranks with Cohere Rerank for the Hub, and drafts and answers with Vertex Gemini 2.5 Flash.

Research and the document-backed Revise and Review workflows attempt to remove identifying details before retrieval. This anonymization is best effort: if it is unavailable or fails, the workflow may continue with the original text. Generate without a document sends its drafting prompt directly to Gemini and skips retrieval, so do not include confidential or identifying information in any field. A separate regex PII firewall re-screens explicitly uploaded Session sources before Supermemory accepts them.

Portfolio demonstration — not legal advice. Do not enter confidential client information.
