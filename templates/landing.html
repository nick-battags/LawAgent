Pipeline

  1. Cohere embed-v4.0 — 1024-dim query & document embeddings
  2. Neon pgvector — vector similarity retrieval from a 22-row playbook corpus
  3. Cohere rerank-v3.5 — cross-encoder re-ranking of top candidates
  4. Gemini 2.5 Flash — generation, issue spotting, missing-clause detection
  5. Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite — PII anonymization for uploaded documents
